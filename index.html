My Story...

I am a Data Analyst with expertise in ERP systems and enterprise application management. I've built my career by combining technical proficiency with strong analytical skills to optimize business processes and drive operational efficiency. I specialize in translating complex business requirements into technical solutions, utilizing tools like SQL, Python, and Power BI to extract insights from enterprise data. Before moving into ERP and data analytics, I earned a B.S. in Ecosystem Science and Policy from the University of Miami, where I developed a strong foundation in analytical thinking and policy research. I also interned at the U.S. Department of Energy, where I digitized and designed a continental U.S. map using ArcMap and co-authored a literature review on energy disparity, justice, and equity in the building sector — experience that shaped how I approach data, systems, and equity in public-facing technology.

I've since built on that foundation with hands-on coursework and certifications in Data Analytics, AI, Python, SQL, and I'm always looking to grow my technical toolkit.

I'm especially interested in opportunities where I can keep deepening my Data Analytics expertise, work with data at scale, and collaborate with teams that value curiosity, inclusion, and continuous learning.
